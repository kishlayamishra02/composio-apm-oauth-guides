    First question: do you need your own OAuth app at all?

    The OAuth app is what your users see on the consent screen. Composio runs one for many apps, so you may not need yours yet.

    Registering an app takes minutes almost everywhere. The difference between an hour and two months is review: what triggers it, what reviewers want, and how long they take.
