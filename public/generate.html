Research an app

The agent identifies the app's developer programme, investigates registration, approval and limits in parallel, writes a guide from what it found, then re-reads the pages it cited to check its own quotes. Usually one to three minutes; you can leave this page open.

  1. Identify the app and its developer programme
  2. Investigate three questions in parallel
    Registration, and what works before approval
    Approval: triggers, requirements, waits
    Scopes, plans, rate limits, recent changes
  3. Write the guide from the findings only
  4. Check its own work: re-read cited pages, probe the developer portal
Live guides are written by a model from public web pages and checked automatically. They are a starting point, not a verified answer: check anything marked uncertain.
